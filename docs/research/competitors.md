# Competitor map: where to borrow ideas

2026-11-26. Web research from app store listings, Product Hunt, the press and each product's own pages. Domain terms follow `GLOSSARY.md`. The lens is "what could filmes borrow"; each idea is cross-checked against what filmes already has (decisions in `AGENTS.md`, issues). `PRODUCT.md` still rules scope: an idea that conflicts with it is marked, not promoted.

**Marks.** _[unverified]_: no source opened in this research confirms it. _[general knowledge]_: known, but no source was opened for it here. Criticker and Does the Dog Die refused direct fetches (403), so their entries come from searches and coverage.

## The short version

- **No one does what filmes does.** Group match apps only intersect yeses from swipes: none says why, none gives a Nota prevista per person, none cares about fairness across Presentes. The products that predict a rating (Criticker, WATCHA PEDIA, Taste.io) are solo, or at most for a couple.
- **The group apps' biggest lesson is speed.** People deciding want an answer in seconds, not a swipe session (the WeWatch post-mortem). Netflix's "Play Something" shows the opposite failure: a random pick with no reason, removed in under two years.
- **The closest threat to the MCP connector** is Tubi's native app inside ChatGPT (April 2026). Hub Entertainment Research (November 2026) says 2 in 5 streaming subscribers already ask ChatGPT or Google AI Mode what to watch.

**ReelMatch.** A trailer, not a poster, is the unit you swipe on. "You both said yes!" card when a friend's liked list overlaps yours. Invite link that rewards both sides.

- Borrow: trailer on the film card; the overlap card.

**Matched** (Taste Labs, same company as Taste.io). Recommendations come from "couples like you": taste neighbors at the pair level, not the person. Matches can be filtered by mood and service; "Up Next" and "Not Interested" labels.

**Criticker.** Each rating is read as a percentile tier of the user's own scale, the same idea as filmes' "Régua por percentil" (#172). TCI (Taste Compatibility Index) says who you agree with most and shows how each TCI was computed; the minimum overlap is configurable (10% to 50%). PSI (Probable Score Index) is the predicted score for each unseen film, from the most compatible users.

- Borrow: TCI between Pessoas, with the math shown.
- Complaints: a user with 105 ratings still found the picks irrelevant.
- https://www.criticker.com/explain

**MovieLens.** Onboarding moved from "rate 15 films" to "pick among groups of films", each group shown as 3 films and 3 tags: a bit less accurate, much less effort.

- Borrow: group-based onboarding for a new Pessoa, next to the Pergunta par a par (#181).
- https://grouplens.org/blog/onboarding-new-users-in-recommender-systems/

**Letterboxd.** Pro filters by the user's services and alerts when a watchlist film reaches one of them (JustWatch data). Compare your watchlist with someone else's. Community lists like "Great Date Night Movies". Curated shelves instead of an algorithmic feed. Complaint: nothing for couples.

- Borrow: the watchlist as a signal. filmes already imports the Letterboxd watchlist but no signal or screen uses it.
- https://letterboxd.com/about/pro/

**IMDb.** The app asks for the user's services. "What to Watch" (Fire TV, 2022) decides for family and friends through mini-games ("Time Machine"). IMDb Labs: "Rating Roulette".

- Borrow: little; the Parents Guide is in section 3.
- https://help.imdb.com/article/imdb/new-features-updates/imdb-labs/GRPSR5FKAP9JC69X

**Trakt.** Collaborative lists, even on the free tier. Rating prompt right after watching. "Month/Year in Review" with streaks and stats. Self-service data export. VIP doubled to US$60/yr in 2025; users call the limits predatory and some left for Simkl.

- Borrow: the export lesson is already met (`export`); a yearly recap is low value for the night.
- https://forums.trakt.tv/t/updating-trakt-limits-for-2026/101592

**Taste.io.** "% match" per title. Link accounts with a partner to see what to watch together. Browse by mood. Complaints: unclear rating scale; Quick Rate shows unknown titles; stale streaming data; both people need many ratings first.

- Borrow: an explicit match % next to each Presente is what the Nota prevista already gives (#263).
- https://apps.apple.com/app/id1361180197

**WATCHA PEDIA.** The predicted rating per user is the product. Users praise its accuracy and the "taste analysis" page.

- Borrow: the taste analysis page for a Pessoa (genres, directors, eras she rates high), each item with its Evidence.
- https://apps.apple.com/app/id644185507

**Kinopoisk.** "Сравнить вкусы" (Feb 2026): taste compatibility with any user. The film card shows which friends rated it high or have it on their watchlist. Announced AI-written pitches under each poster (Nov 2025).

- Borrow: compatibility (same as TCI). Do not borrow the AI pitch (see "Do not copy").
- https://www1.ru/news/2026/02/01/kinopoisk-zapustil-proverku-kinosovmestimosti-i-obnovil-profili-polzovatelei.html

**FilmAffinity.** "Almas Gemelas": your most similar voters, each with an open profile; recommendations are what they rated well and you have not seen; you can ignore a recommendation.

- Borrow: nothing new; it is the Vizinhos de gosto with names on them.
- https://www.filmaffinity.com/mx/site-guide.php

**Filmow.** The Brazilian rating base.

- Borrow: an import from Filmow, if it exports _[unverified]_.
- https://filmow.com/o-que-e-filmow/

**Simkl, Serializd, TasteDive, Likewise.** Simkl imports from 14+ trackers and adds ChatGPT lists. Serializd runs a public, voted feature-request board. TasteDive needs no account; complaint: same results for everyone.

## Do not copy

- **Random pick without a reason** ("Play Something"). filmes' point is the Fact behind each line; Netflix removed it for lack of use.
- **Swipe-only matching.** Intersecting yeses with no Evidence and no fairness is what every group app already does, and their complaints (classics everyone has seen, ignores the genre, does not learn) come from it.
- **Long swipe sessions.** People want an answer in seconds; Votos on 5 films is already the right size.
- **Daily swipe limits, weekly paywalls, "does the subscription cover my partner?"** The most common complaint across the niche.
- **Social feed, friend activity, pairing with strangers** (Plex, FilmMatch). Out of scope (`PRODUCT.md`); the Owner speaks for the others.
- **AI-written pitches under the poster** (Kinopoisk). A line with no Fact behind it breaks the "sem fato" rule; the voice is the video store clerk, not "IA".
- **Recommending what is trending** (JustWatch, TasteDive): the same list for everyone.
- **Ads.**
